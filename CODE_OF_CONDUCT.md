# Code of Conduct

## Pledge
We make this community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone, regardless of race, age, gender, orientation, religion, origin, or other status. Good-faith participation is open to all.

## Encouraged
- Respect the community purpose
- Be kind and honest
- Respect different views
- Take responsibility
- Give/accept feedback
- Repair harm
- Support community well-being

## Restricted
- Harassment
- Insults or character attacks
- Stereotyping/discrimination
- Inappropriate sexualization
- Sharing private info
- Threats or violence
- Anything harming community well-being

Also banned: impersonation, missing credits, spam/promotion, sharing restricted content.

## Enforcement
1. Warning  
2. Temporary limits  
3. Suspension  
4. Permanent ban  

Severity decides the step. Moderators act privately when possible.

## Scope
All community spaces + official representation.
